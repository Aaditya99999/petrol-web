Core Biofuel Products

BIS 15607:2022

BIODIESEL (B100)

Biodiesel is a renewable, biodegradable liquid fuel produced through transesterification. In its pure B100 form, it can be used directly in compression ignition diesel engines or blended as per OMC specifications.

  • Up to 78% CO₂ Emission Reduction
  • Low Sulphur (BS-VI Compliant)
  • Multi-feedstock (UCO, Animal Fat, Tallow)

Every production batch is tested for kinematic viscosity, flash point, water content, and cetane number. Our B100 meets BIS 15607:2022 and IS 1460:2017 standards, ensuring top-tier performance for automotive and industrial sectors.

Crude Glycerine

A natural by-product of biodiesel manufacturing, separated and treated to reach approximately 80% purity. This versatile co-product is essential for multiple industrial supply chains.

Industrial Applications

Pharmaceuticals Animal Feed Biogas Feedstock Textile Softening

From the chemical industry's production of polyols to the textile industry's softening agents, our high-purity crude glycerine meets the demanding needs of India's commercial buyers.
