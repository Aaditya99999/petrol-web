Who We Are

ABOUT US

Shivam Biolumina Pvt Ltd

Shivam Biolumina Pvt Ltd is a renewable energy company actively operating in the biodiesel ecosystem, with core expertise in biodiesel trading, biodiesel plant manufacturing, and fuel supply. We are directly engaged in supplying biodiesel to Public Sector Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) and private sector buyers, while also enabling new entrants and enterprises through end-to-end biodiesel plant manufacturing solutions.

OUR VISION

To accelerate India's clean energy transition by delivering high-efficiency biodiesel plants, reliable fuel supply, and scalable solutions that empower industries, entrepreneurs, and energy partners to adopt sustainable fuels with confidence and profitability.

OUR MISSION

To become a leading biodiesel infrastructure and clean fuel solutions company in India, driving long-term energy security, environmental responsibility, and industrial growth through innovation, scale, and strategic partnerships over the next decade.

We guide businesses from ground zero through tender compliance, acceptance, and final approval.
