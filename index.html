What is Biolumina?

Biolumina represents the intersection of biological innovation and industrial luminescence. It is our proprietary approach to sustainable energy, where we harness the power of organic waste and transform it into high-performance, clean-burning biodiesel.

What Is Biodiesel?

Biodiesel is a renewable, cleaner-burning diesel fuel made from biological sources such as used cooking oil (UCO), non-edible oils, animal fats, and other biomass feedstocks. It helps reduce carbon emissions and pollution compared with fossil diesel.

Our Story

How Is It Made?

Biodiesel is typically produced through transesterification, where fats or oils react with an alcohol (methanol) in the presence of a catalyst to form methyl esters (biodiesel) and glycerin.
